Help & How It Works

Everything you need to know about reporting and tracking issues on Compass.

How is my complaint classified?

An ML model reads your description and assigns a category (Wi-Fi, plumbing, electrical, hostel, safety, other) and routes it to the right department automatically.

What does "anonymous" actually hide?

Your name and email are hidden from the admin and public feed. The issue is still tracked, prioritized, and resolved the exact same way.

Why did my report get merged?

If someone already reported the same issue in a similar location, Compass merges your report into theirs instead of creating duplicate tickets.

How is priority decided?

Priority combines urgency signals in your text, how many students reported the same issue, and how long it has been open.

What are "Predicted issues"?

Compass looks at historical complaint patterns (e.g. AC failures spiking in summer) to flag what's likely to recur soon so admins act proactively.

How do student confidence scores work?

Every time your reported issue is successfully resolved by administration, your Student Confidence Score increases automatically.
