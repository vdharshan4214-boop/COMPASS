The Problem

Right now, campus issues live in four different dead ends.

Scattered Channels

WhatsApp groups, verbal complaints to wardens, and paper registers mean the same issue gets reported 5 different ways — and tracked in none.

No Sense of Priority

A flickering hallway light and a live electrical spark land in the same inbox, with no reliable way to tell admins which needs attention first.

Zero Visibility

Once a student reports something, it disappears into a queue they can't see — no confirmation, no status updates, no closure.

Duplicate Overload

20 students reporting the same broken water pipe creates 20 separate tickets, cluttering admin queues and wasting maintenance hours.

Q 01

Do I need to install anything?

Flip for Answer →

Answer

No. Compass runs in your browser — register with your campus email and you're in. Nothing to download or install.

Q 02

How does the priority score work?

Flip for Answer →

Answer

It ranks issues using ML urgency sentiment, ticket duplicates count, and elapsed time — transparent and easy to explain.

Q 03

What happens after I submit a report?

Flip for Answer →

Answer

It's classified automatically, merged into existing open issues if found, and updated live from Submitted to Resolved.

Q 04

Can I report something anonymously?

Flip for Answer →

Answer

Yes — toggle "report anonymously" on the submission form. It's tracked and routed normally without your identity attached.

Q 05

Is this only for students?

Flip for Answer →

Answer

No — faculty and staff can report through the same dashboard. Campus admins have separate queue management access.
